# Fix next

Issues Sphe hits on the stable Linux prod Edit (nightly-90, 4fa7450f, on mandalorian :7790 / Tailscale :8444). Newest at the bottom; strike through or remove one once it's fixed.

- **Linux tests fail every Linux build, so a release can't pass its Linux check** (Nov 19, run 44527865648 on nightly-90; the same in every `linux-prod-tmp` run since Nov 18). The app runs; the tests are wrong on Linux CI:
  - `apps/server/test/export_ahead.test.ts:305`: no "chunks … already made" line is found in the last 400 log lines after the export made ahead.
  - `apps/server/test/proxy_pieces.test.ts:570`: the PSNR compare of piece 1 reports 31 frames, not 30.
- **"Getting the footage ready" never finishes on a big disk with plenty free** (JJ's Mac, nightly-90, Nov 19, "Greedy Woman…" p1795082283353-113072). Edit keeps free the larger of 15 GB or a tenth of the disk (`storage.ts` `keepFreeBytes`): on JJ's 494 GB disk that's 49 GB, and he had 41 GB free, so every long video's pieces "stream on, the disk too full to keep them" (logged every 15 s) and the dialog sat at "about 2 min" with all 2.0 of 2.0 GB down and 79 of 97 files. It also said "Only 2.0 of the 2.0 GB fits on this Mac", which makes no sense. The tenth-of-the-disk floor is too much on a big disk, the dialog should never wait on files that will only stream, and the room line should say what's really short.
- **Prod Linux Edit froze pages for 10–45 s at a time** (mandalorian, nightly-90, Nov 19 ~18:50, a script page stuck loading). Two causes, both from limits put on the service after the afternoon's freeze. (1) `CPUQuota=400%`: the background text blur's Rust (tokio) threads used the whole quota, and the cgroup was throttled in 90% of periods, the JS main thread with it (the blur's nice 10 can't help under a quota). (2) `MemoryHigh=8G` with the server at ~7.8 GB: ~75k pages a 20 s swapped back in. Raised live to 1600% and 12G/14G; pages load again, but 20–45 s freezes still came about once a minute. Open: why the server holds ~9.5 GB (anon) while blurring two projects, and whether the blur should cap its own threads rather than leaning on the service's limits.
- ~~**A Script import retries two small AVIs all night on a slow line and never lays out the edit**~~ Fixed in nightly-93 (b0f5e3fc): an AVI (anything but MP4/MOV/sound) comes down whole first, a length past its time brings it down then, and keyframes are found in pictures without times. Still open: a big upload giving way to an import's downloads, and an import that keeps failing the same files laying out the rest.
- **"What's beneath title-card" never renders on Linux** (mandalorian, Nov 20–21, "When Cops Rescue Boyfriends…" p1794942765038-42080; 17 times, once at every start). An 8-frame export for what's beneath the effect fails four ways in two seconds: chunked ("the encoder stopped early: … Nothing was written into output file, because at least one of its streams received no packets"), chunked again, whole, and whole "with FFmpeg's encoder". No frame reaches the encoder, so it isn't NVENC alone; a size the encoders refuse (odd, or under NVENC's smallest) is the first thing to look at, and the engine keeps only FFmpeg's last line, which hides the reason. Not seen on a Mac.
- **Planning a long edit is still a synchronous native call** (`exportPlan`, 716 ms on the server's thread for a 2-hour edit, Nov 20). Since 062dc348 it's planned once a revision, by reference; the call itself should be asynchronous in Rust.
- **The engine's own waits have no deadlines** (the Nov 20 export audit): a dead VideoToolbox session or a lost GPU after a lid close can leave a render thread waiting for good. Edit now drops such a job after a minute and starts a fresh one (9680e10d), and says to reopen Edit if a second one hangs; the Rust side should end the job itself (`wait()` settling within seconds of `cancel()`, `recv_timeout` in the stage loops, deadlines on AVAssetWriter's finish and cancel).
- **Edit.app's player** (the Nov 20 playback audit, not yet touched): long videos' sound always streams (it could come from the piece here), the window's read-ahead is skipped for any file that has excerpts, the sound clock isn't restarted after an output device change or a wake, and one failing sound part silences the rest of a play.
- **As Edit starts it answers nothing for 3 to 8 s** on a home with long edits (mandalorian, Nov 20: "the server's thread was held for 3.4 s … last started: GET /api/projects/{id}/text-removal"). Read the `[thread]` lines of a start to see what it is.
- **The hourly cache clean keeps the server's thread about three seconds** on a home of 14,000 cached files (mandalorian, Nov 21: 3.0 to 3.4 s at 00:02, 00:52 and 01:52). The ledger is walked off the thread; what's left on it is `cleanCache` or `cleanExports`. Each now says itself as it starts, so the next `[thread]` line names which.
- **An excerpt cut over the network fetches about twice what it needs** (both machines, bad-day's `no-room`: 98 to 102 MB fetched for 20 MB of excerpts). FFmpeg's reads are open-ended ranges: each cut opens the file twice, drops each read after 2 to 3 MB and reads about 4 MB past the excerpt's end. Bounded ranges would stop it.
