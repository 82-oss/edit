- **"Getting the footage ready" never finishes on a big disk with plenty free** (JJ's Mac, nightly-90, Nov 19, "Greedy Woman…" p1795082283353-113072). Edit keeps free the larger of 15 GB or a tenth of the disk (`storage.ts` `keepFreeBytes`): on JJ's 494 GB disk that's 49 GB, and he had 41 GB free, so every long video's pieces "stream on, the disk too full to keep them" (logged every 15 s) and the dialog sat at "about 2 min" with all 2.0 of 2.0 GB down and 79 of 97 files. It also said "Only 2.0 of the 2.0 GB fits on this Mac", which makes no sense. The tenth-of-the-disk floor is too much on a big disk, the dialog should never wait on files that will only stream, and the room line should say what's really short.
- **Prod Linux Edit froze pages for 10–45 s at a time** (mandalorian, nightly-90, Nov 19 ~18:50, a script page stuck loading). Two causes, both from limits put on the service after the afternoon's freeze. (1) `CPUQuota=400%`: the background text blur's Rust (tokio) threads used the whole quota, and the cgroup was throttled in 90% of periods, the JS main thread with it (the blur's nice 10 can't help under a quota). (2) `MemoryHigh=8G` with the server at ~7.8 GB: ~75k pages a 20 s swapped back in. Raised live to 1600% and 12G/14G; pages load again, but 20–45 s freezes still came about once a minute. Open: why the server holds ~9.5 GB (anon) while blurring two projects, and whether the blur should cap its own threads rather than leaning on the service's limits.
