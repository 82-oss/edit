# Spotting trends, and ideas from outside the Trello board

Sphe, Nov 14: "we really need to think deeply about spotting trends and
getting inspiration outside of our Trello for the ideas. The repack was sort
of like a version 1 of that, but the repack system isn't perfect, and we
probably need to rethink something else here." Picked up on Nov 15. Delete
this file once it's built or dropped.

## Where we are

- An idea starts once someone has already found the case: 36 of the last 40
  prod ideas were a single pasted YouTube link and 4 were articles. Edit then
  writes the titles and the thumbnail guide (repackaging, `docs/ideas.md`).
  Finding the case is still done by hand.
- What already exists:
  - The outlier score: a video's views against its channel's recent median
    (`relay/src/ideas.ts` `outlier`). It's only worked out for the one video
    someone pasted.
  - The board's duplicate check (`ideas/board.ts` `duplicates`).
  - Channel videos, top or recent (`/v1/channels/videos`, the
    `channel_videos` tool).
  - The approved titles, read live.
- Everything to do with YouTube goes through the relay's API key, never the
  team's own IP (Sphe: never risk a user's ban).

## The proposal: Finds, in each channel's ideas lane

A list filled in the background. Each find is a case with a one-line reason
it's on the list, and two buttons: **Make it** (New idea with the link filled
in) and **Skip**.

1. **Watched channels.** Each of our channels gets a list of the competitors
   to watch. Once a day the list is checked for new uploads, and each upload
   is scored on:
   - its outlier score (views against the channel's median);
   - its speed: views in the first 24–48 h against that channel's usual.

   A video at 5× its channel's usual in two days is a trend right now. Cost:
   the uploads playlist and the videos call are 1 unit per 50 videos, so 200
   channels a day is about 400 units of the 10,000 a day we get by default.
   Search costs 100 units, so we'd use it sparingly.
2. **Sources that come before the competitors.** Bodycam and interrogation
   cases start at police departments' own YouTube channels, their press
   releases and the news. Watching those finds a case before other channels
   have covered it, which repackaging can never do.
3. **Grouping and checking.** The same case found across several sources
   becomes one find (several channels covering it is itself a signal).
   Anything already on the board, or already made, is dropped. Each find gets
   its reason, for example: "4 channels covered this, the top one did 6× its
   usual".
4. **Skip teaches it.** A skip with a reason is a lesson for the scout, kept
   the same way as every agent's lessons (`app/apps/server/src/teaching.ts`).
5. **Later: our own numbers.** A week after a video goes out, its views go
   back in, so the scout learns which kinds of cases work for us.

**First step:** watched channels plus the speed score, in the ideas lane,
with Make it and Skip. Measure how many finds turn into ideas that get
approved on the board.
